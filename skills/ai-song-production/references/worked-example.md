# Worked example: "To The Moon" (2026)

A real run of this skill, start to finish, in one session. Use it to calibrate scope, tone and how many rounds to expect.

## Brief
- Director likes high-energy EDM / K-pop mixes. References: ZIAGA "Back Online" (techno, 128 BPM), Mathame "Love Somebody" (melodic techno), Alok & ILLENIUM "To The Moon" (progressive house), BLACKPINK "Jump".
- Shared sound: dark minor-key four-on-the-floor pulse + euphoric supersaw lift + K-pop chant hook.
- ~60 s, English, one female lead, bittersweet tone.

## Concept
- Four directions offered (JUMP CUT, LOW BATTERY, ZERO G, IN SYNC). Director picked ZERO G + the phrase "to the moon", with a satirical angle: the AI/tech bubble everyone knows is about to burst.
- Director rejected "show the bubble literally" and "a club as the main arc", so three new arcs were offered. Picked **Paper Moon**: a modern moon rush. She launches, parties in zero G, lands and plants a flag, and the moon is a paper set under studio lights. She stays anyway. A warning HUD carries the "bubble" signals.
- Knots: "to the moon" (high / hype / launch) and "don't look down" (vertigo / don't check the chart / denial).
- Title flag: a reference track shares the title, so the director was warned about search discoverability.

## Lyric (locked at draft 1)
```
[Intro - filtered pad, soft female whisper close to the mic]
Everybody's going up. You coming?
[Verse - spoken, cool and deadpan, every word crisp, rhymes land on the bar ends]
Sold my car to buy a rocket, put it all on hype.          (13)
Every chart I see is vertical. We must be right.          (13)
Ground control keeps calling, says the air is getting thin. (13)
I put them on mute and turn the music up again.           (13)
[Pre-Drop - sung, rising, long held notes, the beat drops out]
Hold your breath and let go
[Drop - shouted chant hook, a crowd answers each line, euphoric supersaw lift]
TO THE MOON! (to the moon) / DON'T LOOK DOWN! (don't look down) / TO THE MOON! (to the moon) / Nobody's coming down
[Break - no drums, a glassy pad and her voice alone, tender]
It sounds hollow when I land. / I know it's paper. I'm still here.
[Build - riser, the kick returns]   Plant the flag and say we made it
[Final Drop …]  … Never coming down
[Stop - one bar of total silence]   [End - whispered, no music]  Don't look down.
```

## Suno rounds
1. **Round 1** (v6, Advanced, female, duration 1:05): S1 "melodic techno into euphoric dance-pop…" and S2 "hard-hitting K-pop dance track built on melodic techno… bouncy… a little bittersweet". 4 takes, 1:03–1:07. Director liked an S2 take.
2. **Round 2**, delivery note: "make the ground-control lines sound like an intercom / loudspeaker, cheekier, a bit of a laugh". Fix: Remix → Cover of that take, the two lines moved under a new `[Intercom - …]` cue with `(ha-ha)` / `(heh)` ad-libs, audio influence 66%. Director picked the newest cover.
3. **Measurement** of the locked take found Suno had **skipped the break and the build** (the sincere line was gone). The M4A's embedded lyrics and the transcription both confirmed it.
4. **Round 3**: Song Editor → Replace Section on the quiet outro (0:52.4–0:59.6), new break lyrics + a section-only style. Two alternates; director inserted V01 → Commit → Save as New Song. The song grew from 1:04 to 1:14.9 and was fully re-rendered.

## Lessons from this run
- Measure before storyboarding: the skipped break would have broken the story.
- The director moved the wrong file once (the old WAV). Verify name and duration before analysing.
- Whisper misheard "breath" as "glass": a pronunciation flag worth a listen.
- Full-mix beat tracking said 136 BPM with ±80 ms errors; the kick-anchored grid was within ±10 ms.
